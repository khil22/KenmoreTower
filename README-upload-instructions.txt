HOW TO UPLOAD TO GITHUB
1. Open your repository: KenmoreTower
2. Click "Add file" > "Upload files"
3. Drag ALL 8 files above into GitHub (plus your images/ folder).
   IMPORTANT: index.html must be at the top level of the repo, not in a subfolder.
   IMPORTANT: upload styles.css and script.js too, or the pages will look unstyled.
4. Scroll down and click "Commit changes".
5. Settings > Pages > Build and deployment:
   - Source: Deploy from a branch
   - Branch: main
   - Folder: / (root)
6. Save, wait ~1-2 minutes, then open:
   https://khil22.github.io/KenmoreTower/
   (Hard-refresh with Ctrl+Shift+R / Cmd+Shift+R to clear the cache.)
